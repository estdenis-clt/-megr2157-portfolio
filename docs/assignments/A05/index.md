## Assumption sensitivity:


One assumption that I made that I would change if doing this again would be related to the application of this part. Because although my choice of Steel for this project is an extremely versatile and strong material, we do not know exactly what application this is going into. There are a lot of outside factors that could set this bracket from being out of the safety bound with external conditions we were unaware of. I think with such a small part with the tolerances that it does, I think it's important to outline the overall need statement and the parameters of its product life cycle. without knowing how it's going to be used in a real world scenario, we could be leaving out factors that truly mattered such as extreme weather changes, high humidity, exposures to corrosive materials and a long list of others. I think that the assumptions we made that the material is mostly about the dimensions of this part needs a lot to be said about how it would actually perform in the real world. Now as much as this would be nice to have all of this outlined, I do understand that the scope of this project is not that specific and it's supposed to be a good thought process and exercise on how the engineering process works at a high level.
